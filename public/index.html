    Interaction

    • Desktop: hover a course to preview its chain, click to open the details panel.
    • Mobile / iPad: single-tap a course to trace its prerequisite chain, double-tap the same course to open its details.
    • Tap a certificate (Cybersecurity / Cloud) to highlight every course on that path.
    • Switch programs from the sidebar — BS in CS, Minor · CS, or Minor · IT — each shows only its required courses.
    • Drag empty space to pan; pinch or scroll to zoom.
